STRATEGY GEAR

🛡️ Gear 1: Defensive / Survival

• When: Tough index hole, poor lie, out-of-position tee shot, or heavy hazard.
• Rule: Aim for middle of green or widest fairway section. Never attack a tucked pin. Goal is taking double bogey completely off the card.

🎯 Gear 2: Standard Scoring

• When: Normal fairway position, standard approach distance.
• Rule: Play to smart targets and yardage numbers. Priority is entering the Scoring Zone (≤100yd) in regulation and getting down in 3.

🚀 Gear 3: Attack / Green Light

• When: Short par 4 tee shot, reachable par 5, or wedge in hand from fairway.
• Rule: Green light to pin-seek safe flags. Aggressive play allowed only when risk of penalty is minimal.
